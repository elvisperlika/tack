Sticky notes that stick to windows, not your desktop.

Tack pins a note to a folder, a document or a browser tab on your Mac. The note travels with that window and is there every time you come back to it.

A note only helps if you see it at the right moment.

Most reminders are about one specific thing. Rename these files before sending them. This invoice is still unpaid. Don't touch this folder until Friday.

The trouble is where the reminder ends up: on a desktop already covered in other reminders, or inside an app you have to remember to open. So the day you finally open that folder, the note about it is somewhere else.

What you can already use, and where it falls short.

There are plenty of ways to write a note on a Mac. Almost none let you stick it to the thing it's about.

  • Stickies

    Notes float on the desktop. All of them are in view all the time, whether or not they matter right now, and none of them knows which window it belongs to.

  • Apple Notes

    Notes live inside their own app. You have to remember that a note exists, then leave what you are doing to go and find it.

  • Finder comments

    A comment does belong to its folder, but it sits at the bottom of the Get Info panel, where nobody looks. Out of sight on the day you need it.

  • Other sticky-note apps

    More colours and more options, on the same desktop. The pile grows, it covers your work, and you stop reading it.

  • Tack

    The note is pinned to the window it's about. Open the folder and the note is there. Close it and the note is gone with it.

Tack puts the note where the work is.

Tack is a small app that lives in your Mac's menu bar. Open a folder, a document or a web page, add a note, and from then on the two belong together.

A note for every place

Folders
Pin a note to any folder in Finder. Move the folder or copy it somewhere else and the note goes with it.
Documents
A note on a PDF or a text file comes back when you reopen the file, tomorrow or next month.
Browser tabs
In Safari, Chrome and Arc every page keeps its own note. Switch tab and the note switches with you.
Terminal tabs
Each Terminal tab can carry its own note too.
As wide as you like
Choose whether a note shows on one tab, on the whole window, or everywhere in that app.

It stays with its window

Follows along
Move the window and the note moves with it, staying in the same corner where you left it.
There when the window is
The note stays on its window while you work in other apps, and steps aside only when something else covers it. Your desktop stays empty.
Never lost
A note can't be dragged off its window. On a small window it shrinks to fit, and grows back when the window does.

Small and quiet

Lives in the menu bar
No Dock icon and no window of its own. A pin in the menu bar is all you see until you need it.
Updates itself
Tack checks for a new version once a day and asks before installing it. Your notes are left alone.
Yours
Free, open source, and your notes are stored on your Mac.

Pleasant to write in

Formatting as you type
Type **bold**, a # heading or a - list and it is formatted on the spot. There is no toolbar and no symbols are left behind.
Checklists
Type [] for a checkbox, then click it to tick things off.
Four colours, three typefaces
Both are picked from a small dot in the note's corner. Rest the pointer on a choice to preview it.
Gone when empty
Delete a note's text and the note removes itself. There is nothing to tidy up.

All your notes in one view

Everything, grouped by place
Press ⌘O and Tack shows every note you have, each under the folder, document or tab it is pinned to.
Edit right there
Change a note in this view and the one on its window keeps up as you type.
Jump back
A dot on each note brings its window, tab or folder back to the front.

Your first note in a minute.

  1. Open Tack

    Download it, unzip it, then right-click the app and choose Open. Your Mac asks to let Tack see your windows and folders. Say yes and a pin appears in the menu bar.

  2. Go where the note belongs

    Click the folder, the document or the web page you want to leave a note on.

  3. Add the note

    Click the pin, choose Add note here, and type. Drag the note to the corner you like. That's it.

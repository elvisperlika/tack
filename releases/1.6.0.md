# What's new in Tack 1.6.0

## ✨ What's New
- **The dashboard:** a note grows into a view of all your notes, and shrinks back into itself.
- Notes stay on screen when their app loses focus.
- Choose a note's pin level from the pill; the right-click menu is gone.

## 🐛 Fixes
- Finder notes follow their own folder's window, not whichever Finder window is in front.
- A note can't be pinned onto a level that already holds one.
- Bold and italic no longer break list markers.
- Tack waits less on slow apps and reads window positions less often.

# What's new in Tack 1.3.0

## ✨ What's New
- **The hover pill:** a note's controls fold into a small pill that opens when you reach it.
- Colour and delete are dots in the pill, replacing the menu button.
- Notes are edited as blocks, with real list markers and checkboxes.
- A new note starts centred in its window.

## 🐛 Fixes
- Notes are no longer lost when a save fails or is still pending.
- Browser and Terminal notes keep a stable identity across tab changes.
